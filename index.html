Our Services

The Right Care for Your Family Needs

Every service is tailored to the patient's condition. We conduct an initial assessment to ensure the assigned nurse fits the needs.

Elderly Care

Daily assistance for the elderly: feeding, bathing, mobility, and routine health monitoring. Our nurses are trained to handle dementia and other geriatric conditions.

  • Blood pressure & blood sugar monitoring
  • Daily report to family

Post-Op & Hospital Care

Wound care, medication administration as prescribed, and vital signs monitoring. Direct coordination with attending doctor if needed.

  • Bandage changing & wound care
  • Light physiotherapy

Daily Medical Accompaniment

For patients with chronic conditions like diabetes, stroke, or heart disease. Nurses ensure medication schedule, diet, and activities follow doctor's orders.

  • Regular medication management
  • Daily condition logging

Palliative Care

Compassionate support for patients with terminal illnesses. Focus on patient comfort and emotional support for the family.

  • Pain management
  • 24-hour support

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How to ensure the arriving nurse is qualified?

All our nurses are required to have an active license (STR) which we verify directly. Additionally, they pass a strict selection process: background check, competency test, and interview. Before booking, you can see the nurse's full profile including experience and specialization.

How long from booking until the nurse arrives?

For urgent needs, we can send a nurse within 2-4 hours (depending on availability in your area). For scheduled bookings, usually 1-2 working days after assessment is done. We always try as fast as possible without sacrificing proper matching.

What if the nurse isn't a fit or is unavailable?

We understand chemistry is important. If within the first 3 days you feel it's not a fit, we will find a replacement at no extra cost. For unavailable nurses (sick/leave), we always prepare a backup nurse with equivalent qualifications.

Are there any additional costs?

The price we quote is all-in: nurse salary, team coordination, reports, and support. Not included: disposable medical supplies (bandages, needles, etc.) and nurse transport if outside main service area. All extra costs will be informed upfront, no surprises.

How is the payment system?

For visit packages: pay after service is done. For daily and live-in packages: payment in advance per week or month (discount for monthly payment). We accept bank transfers and e-wallets.

Tell us the patient's condition, and we'll help find the right nurse. Free initial consultation, no commitment.
