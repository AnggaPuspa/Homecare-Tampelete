Questions About Services

What if my need is in between 2 services?

That happens often. For example, needing an elderly companion but also having a wound to care for. Later we combine or find a nurse who can handle both. Just chat first, tell us the condition.

Can I change service type after running?

Absolutely. Patient condition can change—sometimes getting better, sometimes needing more intensive care. We are flexible, just coordinate if you want to adjust.

Can I try 1–2 days first?

Yes. We understand if you want to see if it fits or not before committing long term. Daily package available, and if continuing to weekly/monthly there is price adjustment.

What language can the nurses speak?

All our nurses speak Indonesian. Some also speak Balinese and basic English. If there is special language need, just say during initial consultation.
