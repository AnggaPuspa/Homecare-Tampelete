What Our Nurses Do

Not Just Watching, But Truly Caring

We understand finding trustworthy care for parents is hard. That's why our nurses aren't just "there", but actively ensuring health and comfort.

Honest note: If the condition is critical (severe shortness of breath, unconscious, etc.), we still recommend going to ER first. Our nurse can accompany to hospital if needed.

Daily Activities

Help bathing, dressing, eating, and mobility. Including small things: nail trimming, hair combing, chatting to avoid loneliness.

Health Monitoring

Routine check of blood pressure, blood sugar, temperature. Recorded in daily report sent via WhatsApp.

Medication Management

Remind and ensure medicine is taken on time. If many meds, we help organize schedule.

Fall Prevention

Accompany to bathroom, getting out of bed, or walking around house.

Report to Family

Daily update with photos. You know exact condition of parents even while at office.

Common Questions

What if my parents are uncooperative?

We understand, many elderly initially refuse care. Our nurses are trained for slow approach, building trust first. Usually within 1-2 weeks they become comfortable. If truly not a fit, we find a replacement.

Can nurse handle dementia patients?

Yes. We have nurses specially trained for dementia and Alzheimer's. They understand right communication, handling mood swings, and keeping confused/wandering patients safe.

What if nurse is sick or takes leave?

We always prepare backup nurse with equal qualification. For live-in package, nurse gets 4 days off/month and we send replacement. You don't need to find one yourself.
